固定搭配

搭配 释义 例句
peer pressure 同辈压力 Peer pressure can also be a positive force.
social cure 社会治疗 Through what she calls the social cure.
group dynamics 群体动力学 Use the power of group dynamics.
set out to 着手，打算 Sets out to make cigarettes uncool.
recruit…to 招募…去做 Recruits young people to promote safe sex.
take a page from 向…学习 Public-health advocates ought to take a page from advertisers.
exert influence on 对…施加影响 Peer groups exert enormous influence on behavior.
spread through 通过…传播 Spread through networks of friends via social communication.
insist on 坚持 We insist on choosing our own friends.

类别 词汇 释义 用法示例

2012年全国硕士研究生入学统一考试英语试题

Part B Reading Comprehension

Directions : Read the following four texts. Answer the questions below each text by choosing A, B, C or D. Mark your answers on ANSWER SHEET 1. (40 points)

Text 1

[1] Come on — Everybody’s doing it. That whispered message, half invitation and half forcing, is what most of us think of when we hear the words peer pressure. It usually leads to no good — drinking, drugs and casual sex. But in her new book Join the Club, Tina Rosenberg contends that peer pressure can also be a positive force through what she calls the social cure, in which organizations and officials use the power of group dynamics to help individuals improve their lives and possibly the world.

[2] Rosenberg, the recipient of a Pulitzer Prize, offers a host of examples of the social cure in action: In South Carolina, a state-sponsored antismoking program called Rage Against the Haze sets out to make cigarettes uncool. In South Africa, an HIV-prevention initiative known as loveLife recruits young people to promote safe sex among their peers.

[3] The idea seems promising, and Rosenberg is a perceptive observer. Her critique of the lameness of many public-health campaigns is spot-on: they fail to mobilize peer pressure for healthy habits, and they demonstrate a seriously flawed understanding of psychology. “Dare to be different, please don’t smoke!” pleads one billboard campaign aimed at reducing smoking among teenagers — teenagers, who desire nothing more than fitting in. Rosenberg argues convincingly that public-health advocates ought to take a page from advertisers, so skilled at applying peer pressure.

[4] But on the general effectiveness of the social cure, Rosenberg is less persuasive. Join the Club is filled with too much irrelevant detail and not enough exploration of the social and biological factors that make peer pressure so powerful. The most glaring flaw of the social cure as it’s presented here is that it doesn’t work very well for very long. Rage Against the Haze failed once state funding was cut. Evidence that the loveLife program produces lasting changes is limited and mixed.

[5] There’s no doubt that our peer groups exert enormous influence on our behavior. An emerging body of research shows that positive health habits — as well as negative ones — spread through networks of friends via social communication. This is a subtle form of peer pressure: we unconsciously imitate the behavior we see every day.

[6] Far less certain, however, is how successfully experts and bureaucrats can select our peer groups and steer their activities in virtuous directions. It’s like the teacher who breaks up the troublemakers in the back row by pairing them with better-behaved classmates. The tactic never really works. And that’s the problem with a social cure engineered from the outside: in the real world, as in school, we insist on choosing our own friends.

21. According to the first paragraph, peer pressure often emerges as ____

[A] a supplement to the social cure.

[B] a stimulus to group dynamics.

[C] an obstacle to social progress.

[D] a cause of undesirable behaviors.

答案解析

选项 判断 解析
[A] 错误 文中提到”social cure”是利用peer pressure的积极力量，而非peer pressure是social cure的补充，属于偷换概念.
[B] 错误 “group dynamics”是social cure中使用的工具，不是peer pressure产生的结果，属于张冠李戴.
[C] 错误 文中未提及”social progress”，属于无中生有.
[D] 正确 根据第[1]段 “It usually leads to no good — drinking, drugs and casual sex”，peer pressure通常导致不良行为，undesirable behaviors对应文中的”no good — drinking, drugs and casual sex”.

答题技巧 : 注意文中的 转折逻辑 ——首句提到peer pressure通常导致不良行为，但后文转折指出其也可以是积极力量. 本题考查转折前的内容.

22. Rosenberg holds that public-health advocates should ____

[A] recruit professional advertisers.

[B] learn from advertisers’ experience.

[C] stay away from commercial advertisers.

[D] recognize the limitations of advertisements.

答案解析

选项 判断 解析
[A] 错误 文中说的是”take a page from advertisers” ( 向广告商学习 ) ，而非”招募专业广告商”，属于 曲解文意.
[B] 正确 根据第[3]段 “Rosenberg argues convincingly that public-health advocates ought to take a page from advertisers”，take a page from 意为”向…学习”，与选项B同义.
[C] 错误 与原文观点相反，文中建议向广告商学习，而非远离，属于 反向干扰.
[D] 错误 文中未提及广告的局限性，属于 无中生有.

答题技巧 : 注意 习语理解 ——“take a page from” 是固定搭配，意为”借鉴、学习”.

23. In the author’s view, Rosenberg’s book fails to ____

[A] adequately probe social and biological factors.

[B] effectively evade the flaws of the social cure.

[C] illustrate the functions of state funding.

[D] produce a long-lasting social effect.

答案解析

选项 判断 解析
[A] 正确 根据第[4]段 “Join the Club is filled with too much irrelevant detail and not enough exploration of the social and biological factors”，not enough exploration 对应 fail to adequately probe.
[B] 错误 文中提到social cure本身有缺陷，但并未说Rosenberg未能”规避”这些缺陷，属于 偷换概念.
[C] 错误 文中只是提到项目因资金削减失败，并未说书中未能说明国家资金的作用，属于 过度推断.
[D] 错误 文中说的是social cure项目未能产生持久效果，而非Rosenberg的书，属于 张冠李戴.

答题技巧 : 注意 指代关系 ——题目问的是作者对Rosenberg著作的评价，需定位到第4段作者的批评部分.

24. Paragraph 5 shows that our imitation of behaviors ____

[A] is harmful to our networks of friends.

[B] will mislead behavioral studies.

[C] occurs without our realizing it.

[D] can produce negative health habits.

答案解析

选项 判断 解析
[A] 错误 文中提到行为通过社交网络传播，但未说模仿行为对朋友网络有害，属于 无中生有.
[B] 错误 文中未提及行为研究，属于 无中生有.
[C] 正确 根据第[5]段 “we unconsciously imitate the behavior we see every day”，unconsciously 对应 without our realizing it.
[D] 错误 文中提到积极和消极健康习惯都会传播，但选项只说消极习惯，属于 以偏概全.

答题技巧 : 注意 同义词替换 ——“unconsciously” 与 “without realizing it” 是同义表达.

25. The author suggests in the last paragraph that the effect of peer pressure is ____

[A] harmful.

[B] desirable.

[C] profound.

[D] questionable.

答案解析

选项 判断 解析
[A] 错误 文章并未明确说peer pressure是有害的，只是质疑外部操控的有效性，属于 片面理解.
[B] 错误 与作者观点相反，作者对social cure的效果持怀疑态度，属于 反向干扰.
[C] 错误 文中提到peer pressure有巨大影响，但最后一段强调的是外部操控peer pressure的效果值得怀疑，而非影响深远，属于 答非所问.
[D] 正确 根据第[6]段 “Far less certain, however, is how successfully experts and bureaucrats can select our peer groups and steer their activities” 和 “The tactic never really works”，作者对通过外部手段利用peer pressure的效果持怀疑态度，questionable ( 值得怀疑的 ) 准确表达了作者观点.

答题技巧 : 注意 段落主旨 ——最后一段通过比喻 ( 老师安排座位 ) 说明外部操控peer pressure的方法并不奏效，体现作者的质疑态度.
