速查表

固定搭配

搭配 释义 例句
catch up to 赶上，追上 Women are still catching up to men in some spheres.
be susceptible to 易受…影响的 Women are particularly susceptible to developing depression.
in response to 作为对…的反应 Developing depression in response to stress.
compared to 与…相比 Compared to men, women are more susceptible.
affect the stress response 影响压力反应 Sex hormones affect the stress response.
under stress 处于压力下 Females under stress produce more chemicals.
under the same conditions 在相同条件下 Than do males under the same conditions.
cope with 应对，处理 They have so much more to cope with.
be exposed to 暴露于，接触到 Women are exposed to chronic stress.
tend to be 往往是，倾向于 The kinds of things tend to be chronic.
one-shot deals 一次性事件 They tend not to be one-shot deals.
wear-and-tear 磨损，损耗 The wear-and-tear from longer relationships.
give birth to 生孩子 She gave birth to a son.
be determined to 下定决心做某事 She was determined to finish college.
live from paycheck to paycheck 月光族，靠薪水度日 I lived from paycheck to paycheck.
diffuse stress 缓解压力 Finding ways to diffuse stress.

重点词汇

类别 词汇 释义 用法示例
绿色 - 正面/中性 spheres 领域，范围 In some spheres of modern life.
capacity 能力，容量 Their capacity for tolerating stress.
tolerate 忍受，容忍 Tolerating stress may be greater.
cope 应对，处理 Women don’t cope as well.
diffuse 缓解，扩散 Ways to diffuse stress.
obligations 义务，责任 Coping with a lot of obligations.
蓝色 - 医学/科学 susceptible 易受影响的 Particularly susceptible to depression.
depression 抑郁症 Developing depression and anxiety.
anxiety disorders 焦虑症 Developing anxiety disorders.
psychiatrist 精神科医生 Chief psychiatrist at the hospital.

trigger chemicals 触发化学物质 Produce more of the trigger chemicals.
ovaries 卵巢 Female rats had their ovaries removed.
reproductive organs 生殖器官 The female reproductive organs.
黄色 - 程度/频率 chronic 慢性的，长期的 More of a chronic or repeated nature.
repeated 重复的 Chronic or repeated nature.
devastating 毁灭性的 Can be quite devastating.
severe 严重的 Severe chronic stresses.

combat stress 战斗压力 Exposed to combat stress.

写作句型结构

句型 结构 例句
比较句 A be way ahead in… than B Women appear to be way ahead in at least one undesirable category.
强调句 It’s just that… It’s just that they have so much more to cope with.
让步状语 not necessarily that… but… It’s not necessarily that women don’t cope as well. It’s just that…
比较句 A may even be greater than B Their capacity may even be greater than men’s.
结果状语 so… that… They’re dealing with so many more things that they become worn out.
被动语态 be exposed to Women are exposed to more acts of violence.
定语从句 The kinds of… that… The kinds of things that women are exposed to.
分词作状语 主句, + doing/done Adding to a woman’s increased dose of stress chemicals, are her increased opportunities.
强调句 It is… that… It’s the hardest thing to take care of a teenager.
不定式作目的状语 主句 + to do Finding ways to diffuse stress before it threatens your health.

2008年全国硕士研究生入学统一考试英语试题

Part B Reading Comprehension

Directions : Read the following four texts. Answer the questions below each text by choosing A, B, C or D. Mark your answers on ANSWER SHEET 1. (40 points)

Text 1

[1] While still catching up to men in some spheres of modern life, / women appear to be way ahead in at least one undesirable category. / “Women are particularly susceptible to developing depression and anxiety disorders in response to stress compared to men,” / according to Dr. Yehuda, / chief psychiatrist at New York’s Veteran’s Administration Hospital.

[2] Studies of both animals and humans have shown that sex hormones somehow affect the stress response, / causing (分词作结果状语) females under stress to produce more of the trigger chemicals / than do (倒装) males under the same conditions. / In several of the studies, / when stressed-out female rats had their ovaries (the female reproductive organs) removed, / their chemical responses became equal to those of the males.

[3] Adding to a woman’s increased dose of stress chemicals, / are (倒装) her increased “opportunities” for stress. / “It’s not necessarily that women don’t cope as well. / It’s just that they have so much more to cope with,” / says Dr. Yehuda. / “Their capacity for tolerating stress may even be greater than men’s,” / she observes, / “it’s just that they’re dealing with so many more things / that they become worn out from it more visibly and sooner.”

[4] Dr. Yehuda notes another difference between the sexes. / “I think that the kinds of things that women are exposed to tend to be in more of a chronic or repeated nature. / Men go to war and are exposed to combat stress. / Men are exposed to more acts of random physical violence. / The kinds of interpersonal violence that women are exposed to tend to be in domestic situations, / by, unfortunately, parents or other family members, / and they tend not to be one-shot deals. / The wear-and-tear that comes from these longer relationships can be quite devastating.”

[5] Adeline Alvarez married at 18 and gave birth to a son, / but was determined to finish college. / “I struggled a lot to get the college degree. / I was living in so much frustration / that that was my escape, / to go to school, and get ahead and do better.” / Later, her marriage ended and she became a single mother. / “It’s the hardest thing to take care of a teenager, / have a job, / pay the rent, / pay the car payment, / and pay the debt. / I lived from paycheck to paycheck.”

[6] Not everyone experiences the kinds of severe chronic stresses Alvarez describes. / But most women today are coping with a lot of obligations, / with few breaks, / and feeling the strain. / Alvarez’s experience demonstrates the importance of finding ways to diffuse stress / before it threatens your health and your ability to function.

21. Which of the following is true according to the first two paragraphs?

[A] Women are biologically more vulnerable to stress.

[B] Women are still suffering much stress caused by men.

[C] Women are more experienced than men in coping with stress.

[D] Men and women show different inclinations when faced with stress.

答案解析

选项 判断 解析
[A] 正确 根据第[1]段 “Women are particularly susceptible to developing depression and anxiety disorders in response to stress compared to men” 和第[2]段 “sex hormones somehow affect the stress response, causing females under stress to produce more of the trigger chemicals than do males”，女性在 生物学层面 ( 性激素影响压力反应 ) 更容易受压力影响.vulnerable ( 脆弱的 ) 对应文中的 susceptible ( 易受影响的 ).
[B] 错误 文中提到女性承受更多压力，但并未提及压力是”由男性造成的”，属于 无中生有.
[C] 错误 第[3]段提到女性”忍受压力的能力” ( capacity for tolerating stress ) 更强，而非”应对压力的经验” ( experienced in coping with stress ) ，属于 偷换概念.
[D] 错误 虽然男女反应存在差异，但选项”表现出不同倾向”过于笼统，未突出文章核心观点——女性在 生物学上更易受压力影响，属于 表述不准确.

答题技巧 : 本题考查对前两段核心观点的理解. 注意区分”生物学差异”与”社会因素”，正确选项通常会 直接对应文中关键词 ( 如 vulnerable ↔︎ susceptible ).

22. Dr. Yehuda’s research suggests that women

[A] need extra doses of chemicals to handle stress.

[B] have limited capacity for tolerating stress.

[C] are more capable of avoiding stress.

[D] are exposed to more stress.

答案解析

选项 判断 解析
[A] 错误 文中提到女性在压力下 自然产生 更多化学物质，而非”需要额外剂量”来应对压力，属于 曲解文意.
[B] 错误 第[3]段明确指出女性”忍受压力的能力可能比男性更强” ( capacity for tolerating stress may even be greater ) ，选项与原文直接矛盾.
[C] 错误 文中未提及女性”更能避免压力”，属于 无中生有.
[D] 正确 根据第[3]段 “Adding to a woman’s increased dose of stress chemicals, are her increased ‘opportunities’ for stress” 和 “It’s just that they have so much more to cope with”，Dr. Yehuda的核心观点是女性 面临更多的压力源.

答题技巧 : 注意文中的 转折逻辑 ——虽然女性承受压力的能力更强，但因为压力源更多，所以更容易出现压力相关问题.

23. According to Paragraph 4, the stress women confront tends to be

[A] domestic and temporary.

[B] irregular and violent.

[C] durable and frequent.

[D] trivial and random.

答案解析

选项 判断 解析
[A] 错误 女性压力确实多来自”家庭环境” ( domestic situations ) ，但文中强调是 长期的 ( chronic ) ，而非”暂时的” ( temporary ) ，属于 部分正确但整体错误.
[B] 错误 “random physical violence” 描述的是 男性 面临的压力特征，而非女性，属于 张冠李戴.
[C] 正确 根据第[4]段 “the kinds of things that women are exposed to tend to be in more of a chronic or repeated nature” 和 “they tend not to be one-shot deals”，durable ( 持久的 ) 对应 chronic ( 长期的 ) ，frequent ( 频繁的 ) 对应 repeated ( 重复的 ).
[D] 错误 文中明确提到女性压力是”毁灭性的” ( devastating ) ，而非”琐碎的” ( trivial ) ，属于 反向干扰.

答题技巧 : 注意文中的 对比手法 ——将男女面临的压力类型进行对比. 正确选项通常会 直接对应文中关键词的同义词替换.

24. The sentence “I lived from paycheck to paycheck.” (Paragraph 5) shows that

[A] Alvarez cared about nothing but making money.

[B] Alvarez’s salary barely covered her household expenses.

[C] Alvarez got paychecks from different jobs.

[D] Alvarez paid practically everything by check.

答案解析

选项 判断 解析
[A] 错误 文中提到Alvarez努力完成大学学业并照顾孩子，选项”只关心赚钱”与原文 完全不符.
[B] 正确 live from paycheck to paycheck 是英语固定习语，意为”靠薪水度日、月光族”，指收入仅够维持基本生活开支.结合第[5]段描述的经济压力 ( 照顾孩子、工作、付房租、车贷、债务 ) ，准确反映了Alvarez的经济状况.
[C] 错误 文中未提及Alvarez从事多份工作，属于 无中生有.
[D] 错误 将 paycheck 字面理解为”支票”是典型的 望文生义，该短语与支付方式无关.

答题技巧 : 遇到熟词僻义或习语时，需结合上下文理解其 比喻含义，切勿字面直译.

25. Which of the following would be the best title for the text?

[A] Strain of Stress: No Way Out?

[B] Responses to Stress: Gender Difference

[C] Stress Analysis: What Can We Do?

[D] Gender Inequality: Women Under Stress

答案解析

选项 判断 解析
[A] 错误 文章并未强调”无路可走” ( No Way Out ) ，反而在最后一段提到”寻找缓解压力的方法” ( finding ways to diffuse stress ) ，选项与文章基调 相悖.
[B] 正确 文章从四个层面探讨了 性别差异 : (1) 生物学差异 ( 性激素影响压力反应，第[1]-[2]段 ) ；(2) 压力暴露差异 ( 女性面临更多压力源，第[3]段 ) ；(3) 压力性质差异 ( 女性压力更长期重复，第[4]段 ) ；(4) 案例佐证 ( 第[5]段 ).Gender Difference 精准概括全文核心.
[C] 错误 文章重点是 分析性别差异，而非”压力分析与应对建议”，属于偏离主题.
[D] 错误 文章讨论的是 压力反应的性别差异，而非”性别不平等” ( Gender Inequality ) 这一社会议题，属于 概念混淆.

答题技巧 : 主旨大意题需 梳理全文结构，关注各段落的核心论点. 正确标题应能 涵盖文章主要讨论维度，避免过于宽泛或过于狭窄.
